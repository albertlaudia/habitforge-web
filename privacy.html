1. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the HabitForge mobile applications on iOS and Android, this website, and any future HabitForge-branded software. It does not cover third-party services you might reach by tapping a link inside the app — those services have their own policies.

2. Data we collect (and what we do not)

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any contact details
  • An account or login credentials
  • Your habit list, check-ins, or focus session history
  • Device advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID)
  • Location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Crash logs or diagnostics by default

The only data our servers may receive:

  • An anonymous installation event (a randomly generated install ID and a timestamp) if and only if you have enabled analytics in Settings. This event contains no personal data and no habit content.
  • If you email us for support, the contents of your email, used only to resolve your request.

3. How your data is stored

All habit data (the names of your habits, your check-in history, focus session records, and your streak counters) is stored locally on your device inside an encrypted Isar database. On iOS this is protected by the system Data Protection class. On Android it is stored in the app's private internal storage.

Deleting the app from your device permanently removes this data. There is no copy of it on our servers.

4. Third parties

The HabitForge mobile apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or social SDKs by default.

The following services may receive a request from your device under specific, opt-in conditions:

Apple Push Notification service (APNs) / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

Used solely to deliver the daily reminder notifications you have scheduled in the app. No message content is logged or stored by us beyond what is required to schedule the reminder.

Apple App Store / Google Play

Processes the one-time Lifetime purchase and the optional Cosmetic Studio subscription through Apple's StoreKit and Google's Play Billing. We receive only a receipt-validation confirmation that the purchase is valid — no personal information.

Sentry (optional, opt-in)

If you opt in to send crash reports in Settings, anonymized stack traces are sent to Sentry.io to help us find and fix bugs. We configure the SDK to strip device identifiers, user paths, and any habit content before sending.

5. Optional backups

You can export a full JSON backup of your habit data from Settings → Backup & Restore. The file is created locally and shared through your device's standard share sheet — for example, AirDrop, Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or email. We do not upload backups to our own infrastructure. You are responsible for the security of wherever you choose to store the file.

Restoring from a backup replaces the data on your device. We do not retain a copy of the backup on our servers.

6. Optional analytics

The app ships with all analytics turned off. If you choose to opt in (under Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data), we collect:

  • Anonymous event counts (e.g. "the user opened Today 14 times this week")
  • The app version, platform, and locale
  • An opaque, randomly generated install ID that cannot be tied back to you or your device

We never collect habit names, check-in dates, focus durations, or any content that could reveal what you are working on.

7. Children's privacy

HabitForge is not directed at children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information through a support email, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no server-side record of your habits, the strongest control is yours: deleting the app erases the local data. Beyond that, depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any data we hold about you (we will tell you: we hold none, by design)
  • Request that we delete any data we hold about you
  • Opt out of analytics at any time from Settings → Privacy
  • Withdraw consent and stop using the service

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@habitforge.app. We respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way, we will surface a notice inside the app on next launch, along with a one-tap link to the updated policy. We will not silently expand the scope of data we collect.
