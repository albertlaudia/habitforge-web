Frequently asked

Where is my habit data stored?

Only on your device. The app uses an on-device encrypted Isar database. We do not collect your habit list, check-ins, or focus sessions on any server. Deleting the app deletes the local data permanently. You can export a JSON backup from Settings → Backup & Restore any time.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works without any account, email, or login. There is no sign-up flow.

I bought Premium on one device — can I use it on another?

Yes. The Lifetime unlock is tied to your App Store or Play Store account. To restore:

  • iOS: open the App Store → tap your profile icon → tap your name → tap "Purchases" → find HabitForge → tap the cloud download icon.
  • Android: open Play Store → tap your profile icon → tap "Payments & subscriptions" → "Purchases" → find HabitForge → tap "Restore".
  • Inside HabitForge: Me → About HabitForge → Restore purchases.

If restore does not work, email support@habitforge.app with your store receipt number and we will help.

Reminders aren't firing — what should I check?
  • Confirm notifications are allowed for HabitForge in your device's system settings.
  • Open Me → Reminders in the app and use the "Send test reminder in 5 seconds" button to verify the channel is wired up.
  • Make sure the habit has at least one weekday selected (a habit with no scheduled days won't fire).
  • On Android 12 and above, exact alarms may be restricted. The app falls back to approximate scheduling automatically.
How do I move my data to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings → Backup & Restore → Export backup. Share the JSON file to yourself (AirDrop, email, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, etc). On the new phone, install HabitForge, then go to Settings → Backup & Restore → Restore from backup and pick the file. The app will confirm how many habits, check-ins, and focus sessions will be restored before it overwrites the (empty) local data.

Will there be a web version?

Not in v1.0. The app is designed around on-device privacy and Live Activity-style phone integrations, so a web companion is not on the near roadmap. If a web companion is added later, it will be opt-in and end-to-end encrypted between your devices.

Is HabitForge open source?

The mobile app source is not public today. The privacy policy, terms of service, and this support site live at github.com/albertlaudia/habitforge-web.
